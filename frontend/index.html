How RoadGuard AI Works

Simple workflow from video to verified complaint

1

Upload Video or Photo + Location

Record a road video with a matching GPX/CSV GPS track, or take a photo and add its location. Upload to create a session.

2

AI Detection

OpenCV extracts frames. YOLO detects potholes and returns bounding boxes with confidence scores.

3

Map & Review

Timestamps are matched to GPS. View markers on the map. Confirm or reject detections.

4

Generate Complaint

Create a complaint with evidence. Admin reviews and updates status (Pending → Resolved).
